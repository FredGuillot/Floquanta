# Floquanta Corporate Website

This repository contains the standalone corporate website for Floquanta Inc. It presents Floquanta for Business. Floquanta Personal is maintained separately and must remain clearly distinct from the business offering; it is not a trial, lite tier, or upgrade path.

## Durable project guidance

- Preserve static-hosting compatibility. The site should run from plain HTML, CSS, and minimal vanilla JavaScript without a build step.
- Keep the architecture dependency-light and avoid frameworks or external runtime services unless explicitly requested.
- Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, sufficient contrast, and responsive behavior.
- Protect performance and privacy. Do not add analytics, tracking, cookies, advertising, or remote dependencies without explicit instruction.
- Do not invent clients, engagements, team size, offices, partnerships, metrics, certifications, awards, testimonials, or other business claims.
- Do not casually rewrite the approved core copy: “Explore what's possible.”, “Complex systems. Simplified models.”, and “We don't model everything. We model what matters.”
- “Simplified” is central to Floquanta's modeling philosophy: parsimonious, focused, and decision-relevant—not simplistic.
- Lightweight Decision Twins are focused quantitative models designed around a decision, not exhaustive real-time replicas of organizations.
- AI is one selective capability within Floquanta, not the company's identity.
- Keep Floquanta for Business and Floquanta Personal distinct in copy, hierarchy, and calls to action.
- Do not modify or delete files in `references/`; they are design inputs.
